Offer Letter for Future Horizon Works

Dear Candidate,

Future Horizon Works is pleased to offer you employment as a Product Analyst. By accepting this offer, you agree that for a period of twenty-four months after your employment ends, you will not directly or indirectly work for, advise, or assist any business that competes with Future Horizon Works in any market where the company conducts business.

You further agree that all intellectual property, inventions, designs, software, documentation, concepts, derivative works, and other work product created, conceived, reduced to practice, or improved by you during your employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of Future Horizon Works, whether or not such work is created during business hours or using company resources.

Your employment may be terminated by the Company at any time, with or without cause, and without prior notice, if the Company determines in its sole discretion that continued employment is not in its interest.

Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this offer, your employment, or the termination of your employment shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration, and you waive any right to bring or participate in any court action except to enforce an arbitration award.

You must keep strictly confidential all information concerning the Company, its customers, pricing, roadmap, internal policies, and business operations, both during and after employment, and you may not disclose such information without prior written authorization.
